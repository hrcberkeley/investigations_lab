How Investigations Lab's Query Forge works
Systematic Search Sweep

One big query lets the engine collapse years of activity into a single ranked list dominated by the recent and the loud. A systematic search sweep slices your date range into windows small enough to comb through to the bottom — so finishing every window is a defensible claim of coverage, and an empty sweep is evidence of absence, not of a lazy afternoon.

Search Ledger

A private, local record of queries you save or perform. QueryForge stores no result pages and sends this ledger nowhere. Entries are hash-chained so accidental edits, gaps, and reordering can be detected.
