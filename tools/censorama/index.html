⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This is an experimental tool that is not meant to provide full protection against distressing content.

The machine learning models powering censorama are not perfect and will fail to detect bodies (particularly bodies on the floor) and skip some frames, which will expose you to unprocessed content.

About Censorama

Censorama is a browser-based tool designed to help researchers and investigators view potentially distressing video content with reduced visual impact.

It uses YOLO26 (plus legacy YOLO11) and RF-DETR Seg models running in your browser (via ONNX Runtime) to detect and mask people in real-time.

RF-DETR models are loaded from Hugging Face and may take longer to start because they are significantly larger files.

Created by: Guillen Torres, Human Rights Center, Berkeley School of Law.
