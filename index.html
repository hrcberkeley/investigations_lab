Investigations Lab

Ten years of looking carefully

To celebrate ten years of the Lab, we're sharing a starter kit: the methodological guides we use to train our own teams, and a set of easy-to-use tools to go with them, so that other labs can build on our work and start their own.

Explore the kitSee the investigations

Six stages of an investigation, each with worksheets to guide the method and tools to support it.

The worksheets are the heart of the kit. They're the guides we use to teach the method in the Lab, and the tools we've built along the way sit beside them to make some of that work a little easier.

  1. i.

    The method, step by step

    Each worksheet takes one part of an investigation, from framing the question and planning the search to keeping a record and checking what you find, and walks through it in a way that holds up whatever software you end up using.

  2. ii.

    Written for teaching

    They come out of how we train students in the Lab, so they're meant to be worked through together as a team, adapted to your own cases and passed on to the next group.

  3. iii.

    Tools that stay out of the way

    Most of the tools run in your browser, so whatever you load into them stays on your own device. They have clear interfaces and need little or no installation, so nobody has to know how to program to use them.

The tools are prototypes. They work, but treat them as demonstrations of what current technology makes possible, and check their results.
