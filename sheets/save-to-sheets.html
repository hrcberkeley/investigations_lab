Installing the Save to Sheets Extension

Pre-requisites

  • Install a Chromium-based browser. It can be Chrome, Brave, Arc, etc. This is not an endorsement!

  • Create a Google Spreadsheet

Goals:

  • Installing the save-to-sheets extension

Introduction

The purpose of the save-to-theets Chrome extension is to help individual investigators or teams to create a repository of digital resources in a spreadsheet. The extension does nothing apart from reducing the friction of having to collect individual URLs and their metadata and pasting them into a shared spreadsheet.

Installation instructions

Go to the extensions’ Github page. Look for the green button that says “code”. Click it.

Setting up a project

Once the extension is installed, you need to fill out the settings of your first project. By default, projects are called “Project 1, Project 2…”, but you can change their names by clicking on the “Rename” button.

  1. Who are you?

Each entry that you add to the spreadsheet will have attached this data point so you can track who added what. Agree with your teammates whether you’ll go with full names or pseudonyms in case you need to keep your identity separated from the investigation in publicly accessible documents. In the latter case, remember that at least one person in the team should hold an analogous ledger with people and pseudonyms, stored in a safe place.

The ID prefix is also decided by your team, and it’s only needed if you are keeping track of your digital objects across multiple projects. You can leave it empty.

  1. Set up your Google Sheet.

This is the most important (and the only complicated) step. Open a new or existing google spread sheet and look for the “Extensions” menu and the “App Scripts” item.

Now, whenever you want to save a website, you can right click anywhere on the page (be careful that no text is selected, otherwise the extension won’t fire), and run the extension through the menu:

Note: The extension adds a nice “Save” icon to Google results, Tweets, Facebook posts and other pre-defined digital objects, but this may break as platforms change their settings. The most reliable way to use the extension is by using the right click menu.
